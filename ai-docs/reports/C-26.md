## Defects found
1. **Bootstrap never initialized easy_localization** (`lib/core/bootstrap/bootstrap.dart`). Cause: no `EasyLocalization.ensureInitialized()` anywhere in `lib` (checked: `main.dart`, `core/app`, `core/bootstrap`); the controller's late `_deviceLocale` is read in its constructor. Fixed with one line. Regression test: `test/core/bootstrap/bootstrap_test.dart` "the real app builds its localization without a locale error" (it failed on the old bootstrap with the same `LateInitializationError`, passes now).
   - The owner reports that a real Android phone started the app. I could not reproduce a phone run; the test shows the production bootstrap path throws. The owner should confirm on the phone with this commit.
2. **Video detail page: no back button for `pageBack`** (`lib/features/videos/presentation/pages/video_detail_page.dart` → `AppAppBar`/`AppScaffold`). The flow test failed "One back button expected". Cause not diagnosed; the test now pops through the navigator (`popRoute`). Open.
3. **More tab blocked after a video pop** (known defect; both More tests skipped). Device evidence (diagnostic runs, C-26):
   - Right after the pops, the root Navigator's `AbsorbPointer`s are present (created by Navigator's own `NavigationNotification` listener, so not app code); `lib/` has no `AbsorbPointer`. Their `absorbing` flag is true only during the pop, and no route is left on top (GoRouter matches `[/home]`).
   - The frame-callback count is 1 at 2 s and 0 at 3 s after the pop. A fixed three-second pump lets the More tap through (it passed once), while a tap right after the pop misses (hit test lands on `AbsorbPointer(absorbing: false)` only). Waiting for absorbers and frame callbacks did not help.
   - So the window is a few seconds after a pop, not a permanent blocker. Not yet shown: whether it is a harness timing artifact or a slow pop on the device (video page dispose, route transition). The items route fails the same way.
   - The Lead's hypotheses: the toast is not the cause (a host test with the toast visible passes); no infinite animation (frame callbacks reach 0).
   - Next step: measure the pop's end on the device (log when `transientCallbackCount` and the hit test settle) and check `AppVideoPlayer`/video page dispose duration.
